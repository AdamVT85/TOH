Dossier

Fig 1. The Spanish Steps

The Spanish Steps

Landmark

Where Princess Ann falls asleep on the steps and is discovered by Joe Bradley. The steps look virtually identical today.

Visit Today

  • Location: Piazza di Spagna, 00187 Roma
  • Status: Open — sitting on the steps is now prohibited
  • Hours: Always accessible
  • Cost: Free
  • Visit early morning to avoid crowds. The steps were restored in 2016 thanks to Bulgari sponsorship.

Fig 2. Mouth of Truth (Bocca della Verità)

Mouth of Truth (Bocca della Verità)

Landmark

The iconic scene where Joe pretends the Mouth has bitten off his hand, terrifying Princess Ann. Gregory Peck ad-libbed the gag.

Visit Today

  • Location: Piazza della Bocca della Verità, 18, 00186 Roma
  • Status: Open
  • Hours: 9:30 AM – 5:50 PM daily
  • Cost: €2 donation suggested
  • Queue can be long. The marble mask is in the portico of the church of Santa Maria in Cosmedin.

Hotel de Russie
